National Vector-Borne Disease Control Programme

Standard Operating Procedure: Community Malaria Rapid Diagnostic Testing (mRDT)

Guidance Ref: NVBDCP-MAL-SOP-09

1. Target Patient Population

Every individual residing in an endemic district who presents with acute fever (axillary temperature ≥ 37.5°C) without an obvious localized etiology (such as acute otitis media or cellulitis) should be tested using bivalent mRDT.

2. Test Procedure & Interpretation

  • Clean the puncture site with 70% isopropyl alcohol swab and allow to air dry completely.
  • Prick the lateral aspect of the third or fourth finger using a sterile single-use retractable lancet.
  • Collect 5 microliters of capillary blood using the calibrated capillary tube.
  • Apply blood into the sample well, add 4 drops of assay buffer into the buffer well.
  • Read results at exactly 15 minutes. Do not read after 30 minutes to avoid false-positive artifacts.

3. Treatment Decision Algorithm

Positive P. falciparum: Initiate weight-based Artemether-Lumefantrine immediately.

Positive P. vivax: Administer Chloroquine base 25 mg/kg over 3 days followed by supervised 14-day Primaquine therapy (0.25 mg/kg daily) after screening for G6PD deficiency.

Negative Test: Re-evaluate for alternate causes of fever (dengue, typhoid, respiratory tract infection). Do not administer antimalarials blindly.
